`triple-flames.gif` is the original three-flame animation supplied by the
project owner (`248b76eb06-2648-67b9-5d21-d000373e0a.gif`). Its frames
are unchanged. The app displays its red, blue, and green flames directly;
white and violet are CSS tints of the blue flame. The flames are scaled with
nearest-neighbor rendering and aligned to the bottom of the card medallion.
