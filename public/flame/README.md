`triple-flames.gif` is the original three-flame animation supplied by the
project owner (`248b76eb06-2648-67b9-5d21-d000373e0a.gif`). Its frames
are unchanged. `red-flame.png`, `blue-flame.png`, and `green-flame.png` are
animated PNGs cropped from that GIF with the dark background keyed to a real
alpha channel. White and violet are CSS tints of the transparent blue flame.
The flames are scaled with nearest-neighbor rendering and aligned to the
bottom of the card medallion.
